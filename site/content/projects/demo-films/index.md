## Overview

Certinal's sales team reaches out to hospitals. I made the product demo films they use to show what Certinal does, from the first line of the script to the final 4K export.

## The challenge

A hospital buyer won't sit through a long software demo. Each film had to show a real workflow inside the product, clearly and quickly, and look as considered as the new Certinal brand.

Every film needed a script, storyboards, a voiceover, animation and a final edit, and I made all of them myself.

## How I approached it

### Script first
Each film starts from a product recording or a written brief. I turn it into a voiceover script, so every scene has one job and one line to say it.

### Storyboards
I storyboard the film from real product screenshots, with a guiding cursor showing where the viewer should look in each shot.

### The voice
I produce the voiceover with ElevenLabs, so changes to the script can be re-voiced in minutes instead of rebooking a recording.

### Animation and edit
I build and animate the films in After Effects from the storyboard, and deliver final 4K edits. Later script or shot changes go in as targeted edits, without rebuilding the film.

## The outcome

The films are now part of the sales team's outreach to hospitals.
