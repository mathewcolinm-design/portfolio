## The context

Certinal's sales team reaches out to hospitals. I made the product demo films they now use to show what the product does.

## The challenge

Each film needed a script, storyboards, a voiceover, animation and a final 4K edit, and I was making them from start to finish.

## How I approached it

### Script and storyboards
I wrote the scripts and built the storyboards.

### The voice
I produced the voiceover with ElevenLabs.

### Animation and edit
I animated and edited the films in After Effects and delivered final 4K edits.

## The outcome

The films are now part of the sales team's outreach to hospitals.
