## The context

Avitus is a Web3 casino platform with Crash, Coinflip and Dice games, token wagering, rewards and leaderboards. At MATQCODE I designed the whole experience, plus the motion and social work around it.

## The challenge

A game has to be exciting to play and still clear about money: players wager tokens, so stakes, rules and rewards need to be obvious at a glance. The product also had to win attention before launch, from players joining the beta and from investors.

## How I approached it

### Designing the games
I designed the full user experience: the Crash, Coinflip and Dice games, token wagering, rewards and leaderboards.

### Getting it seen
I created motion graphics and social media assets for the product, which generated more than a million impressions.

### Making the case to investors
I designed the investor pitch deck.

## The outcome

The beta reached more than 142,000 waitlist signups and more than 1,300 concurrent active users.
