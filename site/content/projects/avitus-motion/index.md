## The context

As part of the Avitus Casino launch, I created two motion-driven assets designed to generate hype and drive early waitlist signups.

## The challenge

A launch post gets a second or two in someone's feed before they scroll past. The videos had to look native to Web3, land the message fast, and give people a reason to join the waitlist.

## How I approached it

### Story first
I focused on visual storytelling: each video makes one point and lets the motion carry it.

### Web3-native styling
Bold type, warm light and a dark, onchain look that fits the space it was posted in.

### A rhythm made for social
Clean animation rhythm tailored for social platforms, short enough to watch to the end.

## The outcome

The two launch posts passed 400,000 impressions and 130,000 conversions, and the Avitus beta went on to reach more than 142,000 waitlist signups. See them on X: [first launch post](https://x.com/avituslabsxyz/status/1923417593207243109) and [second launch post](https://x.com/avituslabsxyz/status/1929923573277438129).
