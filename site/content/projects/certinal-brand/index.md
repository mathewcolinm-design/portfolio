## The context

Certinal is an eSignature and compliance company. As its Brand & Creative Designer, I led a revamp of the brand with the marketing and leadership teams.

## The challenge

A rebrand only works when it shows up everywhere at once: the logo kit, the brand deck, every section of the website, and the booths people walk past at events. Producing all of that by hand takes a long time.

## How I approached it

### Leading the revamp
I led the brand revamp end to end, working with marketing and leadership.

### Rolling it out
I took the new identity across the logo kit, the brand deck, the website and event booths.

### Scaling with AI
To cover the website, I built an AI-assisted design workflow in Figma that produced on-brand assets at scale.

## The outcome

The workflow delivered more than 850 on-brand website assets across 13 sections of the site.
