## The context

Asva Labs was building Tetra, a decentralized exchange where people trade crypto, forex, stocks and commodities in one place, with cross-chain trading on Hyperliquid, Solana and Arbitrum. I joined as the UI/UX designer and led the product's design end to end, working remotely with engineering and product in an Agile team.

## The challenge

Four asset classes and three chains in one exchange is a lot for one interface to hold. Trading flows are dense and unforgiving: a confusing step can cost someone money, and cross-chain trading adds steps a simple exchange never shows. The team also had to ship production-ready interfaces on schedule without the product drifting into inconsistency.

## How I approached it

### Mapping the flows
I started with user flows and wireframes for cross-chain trading on Hyperliquid, Solana and Arbitrum, then took them to high-fidelity prototypes.

### A system to build on
I built and maintained a Figma design system and component library, so screens stayed consistent and developers had one source to build from. It sped up handoff.

### Testing the hard parts
I ran usability reviews on the complex trading flows to find where people met friction, and fed what I found back into the designs.

### Shipping with the team
Working with engineering and product, I delivered production-ready interfaces on schedule.

## The outcome

Tetra's interfaces were delivered production-ready and on schedule, on a shared design system the team could keep building on.
