## The context

PricePal is a price-comparison app with an integrated AI chatbot, designed to simplify online shopping by finding the best deals across e-commerce sites. It's a personal case study I designed end to end.

## The challenge

Finding the best price means jumping between apps and tabs and comparing as you go. PricePal had to save people that time and effort in one seamless, intuitive app.

## How I approached it

### Mapping the flows
I mapped the app's structure and the user flows first, from onboarding to search and comparison.

### Wireframes
I sketched the key screens as wireframes to settle the layout before any visual design.

### A logo about finding things
The logo represents the idea of "finding" with a shape like a pair of binoculars. The two letter Ps in the name became that shape, a nod to the app's job of helping people discover the best deals.

### The final UI
I designed the home screen, the product feed and the AI chatbot, which answers questions and points people to the best offer.
