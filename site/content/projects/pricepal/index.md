## Overview

PricePal is a price-comparison app with an integrated AI chatbot, designed to simplify online shopping by finding the best deals across e-commerce sites. It's a personal case study I designed end to end, from user flows to the final UI and the brand.

## The challenge

Finding the best price means jumping between shopping apps and tabs, and comparing as you go. PricePal had to save people that time and effort in one seamless, intuitive app, without making comparison feel like work.

## How I approached it

### Mapping the flows
I mapped the app's structure and user flows first, from onboarding to searching, comparing and picking a store.

### Wireframes
I sketched the key screens as low-fidelity wireframes to settle the layout and hierarchy before any visual design.

### A logo about finding things
The logo represents the idea of "finding" with a shape like a pair of binoculars: the two letter Ps in the name became the two lenses, a nod to the app's job of helping people discover the best deals.

### The final UI
I designed the home screen, the product feed and the AI chatbot in one purple accent, so the deals and the actions stand out from the products.

## Design details

- **Home:** search at the top, a carousel of top deals, then "Suggested for you" products with their best price.
- **Product feed:** product cards that show each item with its price at a glance.
- **AI chatbot:** ask for a product and it lists offers from different stores, such as Amazon, Flipkart and Apple, each with its price and a link to buy.
- **App icon:** the binocular mark in white on PricePal purple.
