# Portfolio site (all routes in site/src)

Scope: whole site, Work (home), Experiments, About, Résumé, case studies. Visitor mode: Persuade for Work/About, Read for case studies and Résumé.

Audience and job: hiring managers judging a product designer quickly, then reading one or two case studies. Action: open a case study, then email.

Constraints: no photo of Mathew; no phone number; every claim from the résumé or supplied assets; project visuals arrive later, so placeholders must read as placeholders.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A calm, light, tabbed portfolio in the Tabfolio manner where framed work, not a face or a stat row, carries every page. Refuses the dark-hero-with-big-numbers template and the photo-led intro.

OWN-WORLD: Cool near-white ground, deep navy ink, slate greys. A soft violet-periwinkle-peach light leak behind the header. Work sits on pastel gradient mats with large soft radii. Pill segmented navigation, grey pill secondary buttons, navy pill primary. Manrope throughout at modest sizes. Deep navy bands take over where the work is shown and where people linger: project grid, design galleries, experiments, career history.

STORY: The visitor sees a product designer with SaaS, fintech and Web3 depth plus brand and motion range, scans the framed projects, opens one, reads problem, work and results, and emails.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Pill tab nav centred at top. MC monogram tile top-left of a 1152px column, name at about 44px, two-line tagline, navy "See my work" pill beside a grey "Email me" pill. Top edges of the first two project frames visible at 1440x900.

FORM: Tabfolio-style tabbed portfolio. seed: none (pinned) "Built my portfolio very accurate similar to this [tabfoliopro.framer.ai]". Signature interaction: the active pill glides between tabs, and the page palette crossfades light to navy as the work scrolls in.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
