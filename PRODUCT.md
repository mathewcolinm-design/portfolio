# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Hiring managers, design leads and recruiters reviewing candidates for full-time product design roles. They scan many portfolios quickly, decide within seconds whether to open a case study, then read the ones that earn it to judge process, judgement and results.

## Product Purpose

The personal portfolio of Mathew Colin. It exists to get him shortlisted and interviewed for product design roles. Success means a reviewer understands within one screen what kind of designer he is, opens at least one case study, and reaches out.

## Positioning

A product designer first, with 3+ years across SaaS, healthcare, fintech and Web3, who also brings brand identity and motion skills, and who builds AI-assisted design workflows that let small teams ship more without lowering quality. The combination (product craft plus brand, motion and workflow-building) is the differentiator; product work leads.

## Operating Context

Reviewers arrive from a job application, LinkedIn or a résumé link, mostly on a laptop, sometimes on a phone. They compare several candidates in one sitting.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Pages: one home page in chapters (Work, How I work, Path, Say hello) and one case study page per project.
- Built in React + Vite + Tailwind CSS + Motion, in `site/`. Deployed as a static site.
- The repository is public and auto-pushes after every change.
- No photo of Mathew anywhere on the site (Mathew's explicit choice).
- No phone number on the site; contact is email and LinkedIn.

## Brand Commitments

- Visual reference chosen by Mathew: the Formo Framer template (formo.framer.media). Follow its style (white ground, black ink, Geist, two-tone headings, hairline dividers, grey work tiles) but design an original layout (Mathew asked for a unique layout, not a copy); keep the content column centred. Do not copy its images, copy or template-specific artwork.
- Theme: light mode only, like the reference (Mathew's explicit choice).

## Evidence on Hand

- Résumé content: `resume/build.js` (roles, dates, project bullets, skills, education).
- Confirmed metrics: Avitus 142,000+ waitlist signups, 1,300+ concurrent beta users, 1,000,000+ social impressions; Certinal 850+ on-brand website assets across 13 site sections.
- Project visuals: Mathew has screenshots or mockups for most projects and will supply them. Until then, placeholders must read as placeholders. Never invent screens, data, metrics, testimonials or awards.

## Product Principles

1. Product work leads; brand and motion support the story rather than compete with it.
2. Every claim traces back to the résumé or something Mathew supplied.
3. Make scanning effortless: one screen tells a reviewer who he is and where the work is.
4. Case studies show reasoning (problem, decisions, results), not just final screens.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA: text contrast, visible focus, keyboard navigation, and reduced-motion support.
